Sheet 04

Built to your spec

For lighting brands, contractors and assemblers. Pick each part of the fitting and we put it together, or send you the parts to assemble yourself.

  1. Step 1

    Housing

    Spot, deep cone, trimless, dome, gimbal, track or outdoor body, in white, black or gold finishes.

  2. Step 2

    Light source

    Cree or Bridgelux COB, matched to the wattage and the housing’s heat sink.

  3. Step 3

    Driver

    Philips, Fulham or Osram, non-dimmable, phase-cut or DALI.

  4. Step 4

    Optics

    Reflector or lens for the beam you need, such as 24°, 36° or 60° on deep downlights.

  5. Step 5

    Colour

    2700 K to 6500 K, from warm white to cool daylight.

Sheet 05

Four decades in Vasai

Started in 1984 and run today by Lavesh Hassani, Hans Enterprises supplies LED fittings and drivers to offices, homes, contractors and lighting brands across India.

We began by distributing Philips LED OEM products, including drivers, Fortimo modules and downlights. That work taught us that a fitting is only as reliable as its driver, so when we built our own range we kept branded drivers inside it.

Every vendor we buy from is checked for quality, capacity and on-time delivery before we place an order. Stock is held at our warehouse in Laxmi Industrial Estate, Vasai East, and dispatched by road, air, cargo or sea.
